🏸Beginner Guide to Badminton



Basic Rules

Scoring: First to 21 points wins a game.
Serving: Hit the shuttlecock diagonally into the opponent’s service court.
Faults: Don’t hit the shuttlecock out of bounds or into the net.

Basic Techniques

1. Serving – Hitting the shuttlecock to start the game and send it over the net.
2. Smashing – Hitting the shuttlecock hard and downward to score a point.
3. Footwork – Moving quickly and correctly around the court to reach the shuttlecock.
